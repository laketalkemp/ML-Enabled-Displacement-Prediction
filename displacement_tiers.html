Source: IDMC Global Internal Displacement Database (GIDD), weather-related displacement events 2008–2025, 183 countries. Controls: INFORM hazard exposure subscores, log population, year fixed effects. Total displacement counts are excluded from the main model: they are dominated by China, India, the Philippines, Pakistan, and the United States, where large-scale pre-emptive evacuations inflate records independently of harm. Cross-dataset agreement: IDMC and EM-DAT rank countries alike (Spearman ρ = 0.74).
